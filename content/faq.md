## General
- Q: Why do I still see a placeholder screen?
- A: Add your authorized web client to `assets/client/eaglercraft-1.8.8.html`.

- Q: Can I launch with a prefilled username?
- A: Yes. Example: `launcher.html?username=VoidPlayer`.

## Multiplayer
- Q: Can I auto-connect to a server?
- A: Yes. Use `autoconnect=1` and optional `server=host:port`.

- Q: Can I move my server list to another device?
- A: Yes. Use Export JSON and Import JSON in launcher tools.
